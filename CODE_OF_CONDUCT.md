# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this to be a welcoming learning project for people at every stage of their programming journey. Participate with respect, patience, and curiosity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and constructive when discussing code or learning progress.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, and approaches to solving problems.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect reasonable requests for privacy and personal boundaries.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention
- Sharing someone else's private information without permission
- Deliberately disrupting discussion or excluding others from participation

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the repository maintainer through the repository's contact method. Reports will be handled respectfully and as confidentially as practical.

Contributors who do not follow this code of conduct may have their contributions or access limited.
